# Figure plan and evidence, Edition 4

Seventeen figures, generated by `render_figures.py` and `render_diagrams.py`
from `figure-data/evidence.json` alone, as SVG, vector PDF and 240-dpi PNG.
Captions in the Markdown define the published interpretation. Commissioned
illustrations are briefed separately in `graphics.md` and are never evidence.

Palette (Edition 4): teal `#0F766E` for EON and its subject, indigo `#4C5FD5`
for systems and structure, amber `#E8A33D` for time and the 2025 origin, coral
`#E0605A` for adverse results and SELL. The earlier rust brown is retired.

## Retired

Edition 3's staircase (mixed a different score into one sequence), fading-edge
(no trend test, non-monotone) and decomposition figures; Edition 2's verdict
mix and horizons. Their findings survive as sentences where still supported.

## Deliberate omissions

- Equity curves and "growth of $10,000": no portfolio protocol was tested.
- A famous-names gallery: EON's universe holds only 3 of the 30 Dow companies;
  Figure 13's seeded draw is the honest alternative.
- Company logos: trademarks; ticker tiles carry the same information.
